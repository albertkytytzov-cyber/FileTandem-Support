Privacy Policy

FileTandem moves files directly between devices you control. There is no FileTandem account, advertising, analytics, tracking, telemetry, or developer-operated transfer server.

Effective September 8, 2026 · Developer: FileTandem / albertkytytzov-cyber

Scope

This policy covers FileTandem for macOS and the optional, separately distributed FileTandem Companion for Android. No Android companion is required for the Mac app’s USB ADB, USB MTP, or standard Android Wireless debugging/ADB paths. Android-specific statements apply only when the separate companion is installed.

Data collection and sharing

FileTandem does not collect, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or telemetry SDKs. The developer cannot see the files, filenames, device identifiers, pairing information, or transfer activity processed by the apps.

Files and device metadata

The apps process filenames, folder names, sizes, timestamps, device information, and file contents only on the user’s devices to display folders, preview files, and perform operations the user requests. Files are sent only to the paired destination device or storage provider selected by the user. Companion 1.0 supports reading files from Android to Mac, creating folders, and permanently deleting selected entries. Deleting a selected folder also deletes everything inside it after an explicit irreversible-action confirmation. Companion does not accept Mac-to-Android upload or rename requests.

macOS access

App Sandbox restricts the Mac app to Downloads, folders selected by the user, connected USB devices, the local network when the user enables a wireless feature, and app-managed storage. Finder integration uses Apple’s File Provider framework.

Android access

The Android companion requires Android 10 or later and uses Android’s system folder picker (Storage Access Framework). It can access only folder trees explicitly granted by the user. Grants, exact paired-Mac public-key pins, and preferences are stored locally and can be revoked in the app or Android system settings. Revoking a Mac closes active sessions. The app does not request broad “all files” access, and Android backup is disabled for its pairing and grant state.

Local-network transfers and security

The macOS app’s optional wireless paths communicate on the user’s local network. Standard Wireless debugging uses Android’s ADB feature. FileTandem Companion binds only to an eligible private Wi-Fi or Ethernet address while pairing, Ready mode, or a user-requested transfer is active. Companion sessions use mutually authenticated TLS 1.3 and exact peer-key pinning with no cleartext fallback. FileTandem does not route either connection through a developer-operated relay or cloud service.

Third-party storage providers

If a user selects a folder exposed by a third-party Android document provider, that provider may separately synchronize the selected content according to its own privacy policy. FileTandem does not control that provider or send content to it independently.

Retention and deletion

FileTandem has no server-side account or personal-data record. Local preferences, cached previews, folder grants, and paired-device records remain on the device until the user revokes them, clears app data, or removes the app. Short-lived pairing codes are kept only in memory and expire. Files copied to a user-selected destination remain until the user deletes them. A confirmed Companion delete is permanent on Android; if a folder is selected, its contents are deleted recursively.

Children

FileTandem is a general-purpose utility and is not directed specifically to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children or any other users.

Changes

If FileTandem’s data practices change, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated before the changed version is released. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.

Contact

For a private privacy or support question, email albert.kytytzov@gmail.com. You can also open a public request through FileTandem Support on GitHub. Do not include private files, device serial numbers, passwords, pairing codes, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
